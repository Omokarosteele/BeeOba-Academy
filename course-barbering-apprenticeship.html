Apprenticeship in Barbering

Earn while you learn inside a working barbershop, guided by master barbers and assessed on campus.

Apply for this programme Ask a question
Overview

About this programme.

The Apprenticeship in Barbering is built for students who want to learn the trade inside a live shop from day one. You will be placed with a partner barbershop, mentored by a master barber, and brought back to campus for structured workshops, theory and assessment. It is the fastest route into earning a real income while you qualify.

What you will learn

  • Clipper work, skin fades and taper blending
  • Scissor-over-comb and classic barbering shapes
  • Beard sculpting, line-ups and hot towel shaves
  • Consultation, client care and shop etiquette
  • Hygiene, sterilisation and infection control
  • Traditional Nigerian cuts and tribal pattern design
  • Retail, pricing and daily shop operations
  • Portfolio building and social media promotion

Entry requirements

  • Minimum age of 16 at commencement
  • Basic literacy and numeracy (assessed at interview)
  • A willingness to work in a live barbershop environment
  • Two passport photographs and a valid ID
  • Completed apprenticeship placement form (we help you find a shop)
