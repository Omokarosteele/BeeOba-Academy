Questions

Everything you need to know.

Still unsure about something? Our admissions team is one message away.

Do I need any experience to enrol?

No. Every programme starts from the fundamentals. What matters is commitment, punctuality and a genuine interest in the craft.

What is the difference between an apprenticeship and a certificate?

An apprenticeship places you in a working shop or salon while you study — you earn a stipend and learn on the job over 36–48 months. A Certificate III is full-time campus study with supervised clinic hours, completed in 12 months (18 for international students).

How long are the programmes?

Apprenticeships run 36–48 months. Certificate III programmes run 12 months full-time for domestic students and 18 months for international students.

Can international students apply?

Yes. Our Ikeja campus hosts the international intake, with additional language, orientation and settlement support. International programmes run for 18 months.

What are the entry requirements?

You need to meet the minimum age (16–18 depending on programme), complete a basic literacy and numeracy assessment, provide valid ID and two passport photographs, and attend a short interview. International students also need a valid passport, visa documentation, proof of funds and health cover.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. Certificate programmes can be paid in instalments. Apprenticeship fees may be employer-supported. Speak to admissions for a plan that works for you.

Is accommodation available?

We do not provide student housing directly, but our student support office can connect you with trusted nearby accommodation options in Agbarho, Abraka and Ikeja.

Do you help with job placement?

Yes. Our placement team works with partner salons and barbershops, and we help graduates with portfolios, CVs and introductions. 96% of our graduates are employed within six months.

What do I need to bring on my first day?

Your ID, two passport photographs, a notebook, and closed-toe shoes. Tools and consumables are provided on campus. A full kit list is included in your welcome pack.

Are the certificates recognised?

Our qualifications are aligned to international beauty training standards and are recognised by employers across Nigeria. We also support Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) for experienced practitioners.

Can I visit before enrolling?

Absolutely. Campus tours run Monday to Friday, 9:00am – 4:00pm. Book ahead so a senior instructor can show you around.
