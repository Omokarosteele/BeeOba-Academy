Good to know

  • Free parking is available at all three campuses.
  • Agbarho and Abraka are approximately 45 minutes apart.
  • The Ikeja campus hosts our international student intake.
  • Student clinic appointments can be booked at reception.
  • Accessible entry is available at Agbarho and Ikeja.
