International Students

Certificate III in Hairdressing

An extended, fully supported programme for international students, with additional language, orientation and settlement support.

Apply for this programme Ask a question
Overview

About this programme.

Designed for students travelling to Nigeria to study, this programme delivers the full Certificate III in Hairdressing over 18 months, with extra time for language support, cultural orientation, and supervised clinic hours. Our international student office supports you from application to graduation.

What you will learn

  • All shampoo, scalp and conditioning services
  • Precision cutting and modern styling
  • Full colour and lightening services
  • Chemical relaxing and texturising
  • Braiding, weaving and cornrow artistry
  • Wig installation and maintenance
  • Bridal and gele styling
  • African hair textures and specialist care

Entry requirements

  • Minimum age of 18 at commencement
  • Completed secondary education or equivalent
  • English proficiency (interview or recognised test)
  • Valid international passport and visa documentation
  • Proof of funds and health cover
  • Airport pickup and orientation included
